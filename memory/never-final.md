Never say something is "final" (2026-07-05, during the PR8 north-arbor-support
design that changed three times in one evening: support-lug -> plate -> base
pedestal).

**Why:** design decisions on this project are iterative against photo evidence
and get revised as better crops/reads surface; calling one "final" misstates
the process and reads as pushing back on further correction.

**How to apply:** in docs, comments, summaries and design notes, prefer
"current", "as decided (date)", or just state the decision. When a decision
supersedes another, say which one it supersedes, not that it is final.
