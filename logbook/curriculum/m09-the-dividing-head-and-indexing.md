# M09 — The dividing head and indexing

## Objectives

- Set up a dividing head and trust it
- Index simple divisions from 6 to 120
- Return to a datum and co-phase multiple parts

## Prerequisites

- m07

## References

- `references/gears-and-gear-cutting/gears-and-gear-cutting.pdf`
- `references/machinerys-handbook/` (indexing tables)
- `cad/scripts/build_cylinder_gear.py` (the +Y notch datum)

## Practice

- How a dividing head works; ratio, plates, sector arms
- Simple indexing for every division this machine needs: 6, 12, 18 ... 120
- Setting a datum you can return to tomorrow
- Backlash in the index: always approach from one direction
- Co-phasing: making twenty parts share one angular reference

## Now make — the real part this unlocks

The **0.4 mm alignment notch** on the `cylinder-gear`s — then prove you can repeat the phase on a second part.

## Competency check

Two parts, cut on different days, whose notches are within 0.5° of the same angular datum.

## Notes

**Blocked: no dividing head yet.** This is on the critical path for M10 and for the whole gear train — buy it early. The 0.4 mm slitting saw for the notch will grab if the feed stalls.
