# M07 — Mill II — profiling, slots, workholding

## Objectives

- Mill a 2D profile to a line
- Cut a slot to width
- Hold twenty identical parts identically

## Prerequisites

- m06

## References

- `references/machining-for-hobbyists-getting-started/09_chapter-7-milling-machines.pdf`
- `docs/machining-dfm.md` (`rocker-arm`, `connecting-rod` rows)
- `cad/scripts/build_rocker_arm.py`

## Practice

- Profile milling: cutter comp by hand, leaving a finishing pass
- Arcs on a manual machine: rotary table, template, or file-to-line
- Slots: width, and why a slot is never the cutter diameter
- Simple fixtures, stops, and soft jaws
- Twenty identical profiles without CNC

## Now make — the real part this unlocks

**`rocker-arm`** ×20 (2.5 mm steel, R800 concave top edge — the **outline** of the plate, not a pocket) and **`connecting-rod`** ×20 (flat 2D profile, book-accurate rough finish).

## Competency check

Twenty rocker arms whose pivot-to-pin distance varies by less than 0.05 mm across the set.

## Notes

The R800 arc is what the amplitude bar rides on. Its consistency across twenty parts is a functional requirement, not cosmetic.
