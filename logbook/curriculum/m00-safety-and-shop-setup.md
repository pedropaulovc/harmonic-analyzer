# M00 — Safety and shop setup

## Objectives

- Set up the shop so the machines are usable without improvising every time
- Know the hazards of every operation in this project before meeting them
- Establish a routine: clean, oil, check tram/alignment, put tools back

## Prerequisites

- A mill, a lathe, and somewhere to put them

## References

- `references/machining-for-hobbyists-getting-started/03_chapter-1-machine-shop-overview.pdf`
- PM-1127VF-LB manufacturer manual: unresolved pending build-year/nameplate
  verification and a matching manufacturer manual. Use the
  [shop inventory](../../cad/process/shop/inventory.toml) for installed equipment.
- `references/jet-bd-920n-operators-manual.pdf`, historical reference for the former JET only
- `cad/docs/machining-dfm.md` — the fragile-feature list is the hazard list

The [archived 2015 PM-1127VF-LB manual](https://archive.org/download/pm-1440-gs-v-3-2020-10/Precision%20Matthews/PM-1127VF-LB-Manual-2015.pdf)
covers the earlier DC-motor version, not the VFD configuration recorded from
catalogue data in the inventory. Do not use it as the installed lathe's manual
unless nameplate/build identification and Precision Matthews confirmation
establish that it applies. Do not use the former JET manual for the installed
lathe's setup, controls or lubrication instructions. Resolve the matching
manufacturer manual before doing machine-specific setup or operation on the lathe.

## Practice

- Level and bolt down both machines
- Lubrication points and schedule for each, written on a card at the machine
- Lighting, chip control, eye protection, sleeves and gloves policy (no gloves at a lathe)
- Emergency stop reachable from where you actually stand
- Fire: what to do about a magnesium-free but oil-soaked chip pan

## Controlled mistakes

Make each on scrap first, under the
[rules](README.md#the-twist-break-it-on-purpose-first), then do it right and
compare.

- **The e-stop out of reach.** Stand where you actually work on the next job
  and time how long it takes to hit the stop. Move the stop or the pendant
  until it is under your hand, then time it again.
- **Chips left to pile.** Run one job without clearing chips and see where
  they go: the ways, the vise, the floor, your shoes. Then set up chip control
  and run the same job again.
- **No routine.** Start a session without the clean, oil and check card and
  note what you had to stop and look for. On 2026-10-29 time went on hunting
  for a wrench.

The dangerous mistakes in this project are not staged: gloves at the lathe, a
chuck key left in, work held by a burr. They are described here and never
made on purpose.
