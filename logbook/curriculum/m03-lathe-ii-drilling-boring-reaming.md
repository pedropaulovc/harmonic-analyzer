# M03 — Lathe II — drilling, boring, reaming

## Objectives

- Put a hole where you want it, at the size you want
- Bore to a running fit
- Ream to size

## Prerequisites

- m02

## References

- `references/machining-for-hobbyists-getting-started/06_chapter-4-drill-presses.pdf`
- `references/machining-for-hobbyists-getting-started/08_chapter-6-working-on-lathe.pdf`
- `references/machinerys-handbook/` (drill sizes, reaming allowances)

## Practice

- Centre drilling and why it is not optional
- Drilling on the lathe vs the mill
- Boring bar selection, deflection, spring passes
- Reaming allowance, speed and why you never reverse a reamer
- The 0.15 mm **diametral** running clearance used throughout this machine
  (Ø6.5 bore on a Ø6.35 shaft — 0.075 mm per side). Get this distinction right
  before you bore anything; halving it is a seized fit and doubling it is slop.

## Now make — the real part this unlocks

One **`pivot-bushing`**: Ø10.0 OD, Ø6.5 through bore, running on the Ø6.35 pivot shaft.

## Competency check

A bushing that turns freely on its shaft with no perceptible rock, measured not guessed.

## Notes

Boring to a *fit* is a different skill from boring to a *number*. Both are needed here.
