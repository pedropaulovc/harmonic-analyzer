# M04 — Lathe III — parting to length, and batch work

## Objectives

- Part off without the tool digging in
- Part to a LENGTH, repeatably, dozens of times
- Understand accumulated error across a set

## Prerequisites

- m03

## References

- `references/machining-for-hobbyists-getting-started/08_chapter-6-working-on-lathe.pdf`
- `docs/machining-dfm.md` (pivot-bushing / lever-bushing rows)

## Practice

- Parting tool geometry, height, and rigidity — why parting scares beginners
- The collet stop: the whole trick for repeat length
- Measuring a stack rather than a part
- Batch discipline: one setup, one session, no mid-run tool changes
- Recovering (or not) from an undersize part

## Now make — the real part this unlocks

The full sets: **19× `pivot-bushing` at 4.5565 mm** and **19× `lever-bushing` at 4.0565 mm**. Do not mix the sets.

## Competency check

Nineteen bushings whose stacked length is within ±0.1 mm of 19 × nominal, and no single part outside ±0.02 mm.

## Notes

**This module decides whether the twenty channels line up.** Bushing length sets the 7.0565 mm channel pitch and the error accumulates nineteen times. It is the highest-leverage lathe skill in the project.
