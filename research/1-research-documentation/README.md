- **Mechanical Principles Documentation**

- **Detailed Breakdown of Mechanisms**
  - Identify and document the specific functions and interactions of each key mechanism:
    - **Crank:** Function in providing motive power.
    - **Cone Gear Set:** Role in frequency translation.
    - **Cylinder Gear Set:** Conversion to sinusoidal oscillations.
    - **Rocker Arms:** Translating oscillations into sinusoidal waveforms.
    - **Amplitude Bars:** Adjusting and defining weighting of waveforms.
    - **Summing Lever and Springs:** Aggregating weighted motions.
    - **Magnifying Mechanism:** Scaling motion for practical output.
    - **Platen and Pen Mechanism:** Recording and visualization.
