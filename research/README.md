The analyzer is described in details in the PDF book and YouTube videos transcripts included in the documents. # Study and Implementation Plan: Recreating Albert Michelson's Harmonic Analyzer

## Phase 1: Research and Documentation (2 weeks)
	* Review provided PDF and video transcripts thoroughly.
	* Document the mechanical principles, gear ratios, and mechanisms (e.g., cone gear sets, cylinder gear sets, rocker arms).
	* Create a detailed component list and identify precise manufacturing methods.

## Phase 2: Preliminary Design (3 weeks)
	* Select appropriate CAD software (e.g., SOLIDWORKS, Autodesk Inventor).
	* Initial CAD modeling of critical components (gears, levers, springs, cams).
	* Identify potential suppliers for specialized parts (springs, precision gears).

## Phase 3: Detailed Design and CAD Finalization (4 weeks)
	* Complete CAD models with high fidelity, including annotations and tolerances.
	* Conduct simulations to validate mechanical interactions and performance.
	* Prepare manufacturing blueprints and assembly instructions.

## Phase 4: Manufacturing and Procurement (6 weeks)
	* Engage professional machining or additive manufacturing (metal 3D printing) vendors.
	* Source off-the-shelf components and materials.
	* Supervise quality control checks of critical parts (gears, cams, rocker arms).

## Phase 5: Assembly and Calibration (4 weeks)
	* Assemble mechanical components following CAD models and instructions.
	* Iteratively calibrate and adjust (e.g., amplitude bars, summing levers, pen mechanism).
	* Document procedures and identify potential mechanical adjustments or improvements.

## Phase 6: Testing and Validation (3 weeks)
	* Conduct thorough testing to replicate documented Fourier analysis and synthesis results.
	* Adjust and refine based on observed performance and historical comparisons.
	* Document performance metrics and troubleshooting methods.

## Phase 7: Documentation and Publication (2 weeks)
	* Prepare comprehensive project documentation including assembly guides, CAD files, and calibration instructions.
	* Publish findings and resources on GitHub for educational and collaborative purposes.
	* Estimated Total Duration: 24 weeks (6 months)
	* Regular milestones and progress reviews every two weeks to ensure project alignment and quality assurance.
