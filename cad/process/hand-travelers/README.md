# Hand-written travelers

Interim shop sheets for the oblique-trammel jig parts. They were written by hand and are not generated or checked by prechips. Print one from a browser at 100% scale. Each sheet names its drawing revision and sources at the bottom. The base bar has its 2026-10-27 and 2026-10-30 versions. The feed lever (TL-26) and end bracket (TL-28) follow the decimal-inch drawings from PR #2112.

The `prechips:<stem>` travelers (pro and hobby) rendered from `cad/process/<stem>/plan.toml` replace these. All three parts' plans have their setup diagrams and pass both profiles (the feed lever from prechips 0.11.0). The generated end-bracket route holds both brackets in the vise at once, one at each jaw end (Pedro's 2026-11-10 ruling), where the 2026-11-02 hand sheet holds one at a time. Every hand sheet is kept until Pedro has compared the generated travelers with it and approved them (owner decision 2026-11-10); delete a sheet only after that approval.

Each hand sheet follows its own, older drawing; the current drawings (v49) differ, and the generated travelers follow v49:

- Base bar 2026-10-27 (v46) and 2026-10-30 (v47): millimetre sheets with seven rail holes drilled at REF stations, eight #9 bracket holes on both bars and slot starter drills. v49 has eight rail holes drilled at the centres measured on each bar's own trimmed rail, four #12 bracket holes (Ø.189, counterbored Ø.313 ↓.200 far side) on bar 1 only, bar 2 with its seats swapped, .47 thickness, 5.50 width and slots cut by the 1/2 in end mill alone.
- End bracket 2026-11-02 (v47): four #8-32 holes from the bar side and two #6-32 from the KP08 side, each tapped about 5/8 in. v49 has two shared-axis #8-32 holes (#29 through) at 13.925 and 55.925 mm, tapped from both faces to full-thread zones .370–.496 (BACK FACE, the bar side) and .429–.555 (FRONT FACE, the KP08 side); a 3/4 in check screw must not run on into the other face's thread.
- Feed lever 2026-11-02 (v47): holes Ø.344 THRU (.344–.348) drilled 11/32, which v49 still prints. After the 2026-11-13 ruling the drawing prints Ø.3438 +.004/0, the 11/32 drill's own size.
