# Hand-written travelers

Interim shop sheets for the oblique-trammel jig parts. They were written by hand and are not generated or checked by prechips. Print one from a browser at 100% scale. Each sheet names its drawing revision and sources at the bottom. The base bar has its 2026-10-27 and 2026-10-30 versions. The feed lever (TL-26) and end bracket (TL-28) follow the decimal-inch drawings from PR #2112.

The `prechips:<stem>` travelers (pro and hobby) rendered from `cad/process/<stem>/plan.toml` replace these. Under prechips 0.10 the base bar and end bracket have their setup diagrams and pass both profiles; the feed lever's plan is held (see `cad/process/README.md`). The generated end-bracket route holds both brackets in the vise at once, one at each jaw end (Pedro's 2026-11-10 ruling), where the 2026-11-02 hand sheet holds one at a time. Every hand sheet is kept until Pedro has compared the generated travelers with it and approved them (owner decision 2026-11-10); delete a sheet only after that approval.
