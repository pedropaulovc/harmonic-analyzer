BEFORE YOU START

  • Safety glasses on. No gloves, rings or loose sleeves near the spindle. Aluminium chips are razor sharp. Clear them with a brush, never your hand, and only with the spindle stopped.
  • The holes are near one end, and that end is the ZERO END. Both hole positions (.28 and .91) are measured from it. Mark it as soon as you've milled it.
  • The long side against the fixed jaw is the ZERO EDGE. The hole row is measured .50 from it. The bar is 1 in wide, so that's the middle.
  • The bar is much longer than the vise jaws, so it's held in two positions. In Setup A the zero end sticks out on the left. In Setup B you slide it so the grip end sticks out on the right. Keep the zero edge against the fixed jaw both times.
  • About 5 in of bar hangs out of the vise. Don't lean on it, and don't cut anything out there.
  • Lessons from 10-30, built into this sheet: measure before you cut and write the allowance down. Cut to the DRO numbers on this sheet, and don't count passes. Conventional milling only: on the left end feed toward you, on the right end feed away from you.
  • Escape drill: if it rings or grabs, hit stop, wait for all motion to stop, then raise Z. Never back off sideways with the cutter in the cut.
  • Collet grip: wipe the oil off the collet and the shank, tighten the drawbar properly, and draw a marker line on the shank at the collet face. If the line moves, stop.

  3. File off the saw burrs. Measure the blank and write it here: ________ . It must be at least 11.85. The ends will take off (that − 11.80) in total.

Setup A: Mill vise. Zero end, zero and both holes.

This is what you should see from where you stand, looking down at the vise. The zero corner is back-left. X is measured to the right from the zero end. Y is measured toward you from the zero edge. Every number below is a DRO reading from that corner.

A1. Load the bar

  1. Wipe the vise floor, the jaws and both 1 in parallels. Put one parallel against each jaw.
  2. Lay the bar flat on the parallels (the 1 in face up). Pick one long side as the ZERO EDGE and put it against the fixed (back) jaw.
  3. Slide the bar so about .4 in sticks out past the left side of the jaws. That end becomes the ZERO END. The rest of the bar sticks out to the right.
  4. Tighten the vise. Tap the bar down with the dead-blow hammer, then tighten again. If you can slide either parallel with your fingers, tap again until you can't.

A2. Check the DRO

  1. The DRO must be in inch mode.
  2. Moving the spindle to the right over the bar, X must count up. Moving the spindle toward you, Y must count up. If either one counts down, reverse that axis in the DRO setup. Every number below assumes both count up.

A3. Clean up the zero end (1/2 in end mill, 1250 rpm)

  1. Spindle off: lower the cutter to about .04 below the bottom of the bar, beside the left end, where there's nothing under it. Lock the head.
  2. Start left of the end, toward the back. Move in .02 in X, then feed toward you across the end. Come back out, move in another .02, and repeat.
  3. Stop when the whole end face is clean. Don't take off more than half of the allowance you wrote in Op 10.
  4. Write ZERO END on the top face, next to this end, and ZERO EDGE next to the fixed jaw.

A4. Set zero (edge finder, 1000 rpm)

  1. X zero: touch the edge finder off the left end face, at about the middle of the width. Set X = −.100.
  2. Y zero: raise it and move right to about X 8.0, past the right side of the jaws where the bar is in the open. Touch off the zero edge from behind. Set Y = −.100.
  3. Check: go to X .000, Y .000. The tip should sit right over the back-left corner.

A5 and A6. Spot and drill both holes (drill chuck)

Move to each position and stop exactly on the number. Brush on oil.

Setup B: Same vise, bar slid left. Cut the grip end to length.

B1. Move the bar

  1. Loosen the vise. Keep the ZERO EDGE against the fixed jaw and slide the bar left until the grip end sticks out about .4 in past the right side of the jaws. The zero end is now about 5 in out on the left, in the open.
  2. Tighten, tap the bar down, and tighten again.

B2. Find X again (edge finder, 1000 rpm). Don't touch Y.

  1. Touch the edge finder off the zero end (out on the left) from the left. Set X = −.100.

B3. Cut the grip end to length (1/2 in end mill, 1250 rpm)

  1. Measure the end mill across its cutting edges with calipers and call it D, normally .500. Your last pass is at X = 11.800 + D/2: X 12.050 for D = .500. Write yours here: ________ .
  2. Spindle off: lower the cutter to about .04 below the bottom of the bar, beside the right end. Lock the head.
  3. Use .02 in passes across the end, feeding away from you (conventional on the right-hand end). Work in toward your last-pass X. Don't go past it.
  4. Check: touch the edge finder on the right end. It should read 11.900, which is 11.800 + .100 (anything from 11.800 to 12.000 is OK). Then take the bar out.

Op 40: Round the two grip-end corners to R.2 (bench)

The two corners at the grip end get a .2 in radius, so the lever doesn't dig into your hand. These are the vertical edges you see from above, through the whole 1/2 in thickness. Anything from R.1 to R.3 is OK, so this is filing work, not machining.

  1. Dykem the grip end on the top face. With the rule and scriber, scribe a line .20 from the end across the top face, and a line .20 in from each long side, running back about 1/2 in from the end. Each corner's radius has to stay inside these lines.
  2. Hold the lever upright in the bench vise, in soft jaws, with the grip end up and just above the jaws.
  3. File each corner flat at 45° until the flat just reaches both lines. Then roll the file round the corner with light strokes until the flat blends into a smooth curve that just touches both lines.
  4. Check against the 1:1 template on the last page. Lay the lever's top face on the paper with its end and long side on the template's lines. Each corner should sit between the blue (R.1) and red (R.3) dashed arcs, close to the black one.

Op 50: Deburr (bench)

  1. Run the deburring tool lightly round every edge, including both ends of both holes: one or two passes, .010 max.

Problems / notes

Template: grip-end corner, 1:1

Print at 100% (Actual size), not "Fit to page". Before you use it, check the bar below the template with your calipers: it must be 1.000 in. If it isn't, the print is scaled and the template is no good.
