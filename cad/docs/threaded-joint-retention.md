# Threaded-joint retention

Design reference for drawing-guidelines.md rule 23 (lock threaded joints
exposed to operating loads): one row per threaded joint of every machine
assembly, with what loads it and what stops it unscrewing. Identical
pattern instances share a row; `Qty` is per machine (`per channel` rows
multiply by the built channel count).

This table replaced `cad/scripts/joint_retention.py` and
`check:joint_retention` (#2883). That audit never failed a build
(`Enforcement.AUDIT`) and every threaded-hardware change had to edit it
(211 commits since 2026-10-01). Joint IDs (`<assembly>/<joint>`) and ruling
IDs (`U-...-no-lock`) are unchanged, so the config `reason:` strings,
spec comments and issues that cite them still resolve here. When a
threaded joint is added, changed or locked, update its row in the same PR.

Exposure: `static clamp` = no operating torque about the screw axis (the
reason says why); `rotating drag` = carries or sits in the drag path of a
member turning one way; `oscillating` = the torque on it reverses;
`adjuster` = the thread's own rotation sets a position, gap or preload.
Threadlocker alone, a seated thread, shoulder or shim is not a lock.

Axial capture: threads into the through-to-bore 1/4-20 GooseneckTap through the east-rail hub boss's Ø10 spot face (1.91D full thread at the printed worst case); square head standing proud outboard; cup point bears radially on the gooseneck's bare Ø12.70 4130-N leg in the 33/64 (Ø.5156) hub bore, no sleeve (owner rulings 2026-11-08 (Pedro), #2315; as modelled it stands 0.21 clear), tightened snug to 9 in-lb (1 N·m) MAX so the .065 wall is not crushed.
- `cone-pivot-screw-in-base`: MHA-DT-20, carrying the whole cone set and crank, swings on the oiled shoulder out to the MHA-SH-VN-52 stop and back at every zeroing (CHECKS 4/5), dragging the shoulder about the screw axis one way then the other; in running, the crank and mesh reaction on the knob-clamped platform also bears on the shoulder. Axial capture: shoulder bottomed on the base top, thread tail in the through #10-24 UNC-2B base seat; head stands 0.25 above the plate (no head clamp on the platform).
- `cone-lock-knob-in-base`: Hand-set operator clamp of a stationary setting: tightened with the cone set engaged and loosened only to swing it (CHECKS 4); in running the platform does not move, and its mesh reaction reaches the stud across its axis (stud in the platform notch) and as friction under the head, not as torque about the stud axis. Axial capture: collarless knurled head bears on the MHA-DT-20 top; stud through the platform notch into the through 1/4-20 UNC-2B base seat.
- `swing-stop-screw-in-base`: Screwed fully home with its head seated on the base top and carrying no member: engaged, the platform edge stands >= 2.0 clear of it; at zeroing the swung platform's edge only bears on the side of the head, across the screw axis. Axial capture: head seated on the base top; thread in the through #4-40 base seat.
- `spring-foot-screw-in-base`: Clamps the MHA-DT-24 foot pad to the base. The spring's load lies in the strap swing plane through the screw axis (blade preloaded on the parked MHA-DT-14 flank, deflected further only while the pinion is engaged for zeroing), so it bends the foot about the bank axis and reaches the screw as tension and shear, not as torque about its vertical axis. Axial capture: narrow fillister head on the spring foot pad through its #4 clearance hole into the transferred through #4-40 UNC-2B base seat.
- `arbor-pedestal-hold-down`: Clamps each cast MHA-DT-02 pedestal's ledge to base MHA-FR-01; neither moves in operation (the arbor is fixed by MHA-SH-VN-54). The gears' journal drag acts about the horizontal arbor axis, perpendicular to this vertical screw, so it reaches the screw only as tension/shear through the foot; no member rotates on or about the screw axis. Axial capture: fillister head on the pedestal's 18-long ledge through a #8 close-clearance hole; transferred through-tapped #8-32 UNC-2B deck seat in 13 mm nominal skin; minimum checked source-model full-form overlap 10.10 mm (>=1.5D).
- `pinion-block-hold-down`: Two screws clamp each MHA-DT-18 pivot block to base MHA-FR-01; the blocks never move in operation. The rocking pivot shaft and lift rod turn in the blocks about horizontal axes along the bank, perpendicular to these vertical screws, and the two-screw spacing carries any yaw as a shear couple, so no operating torque acts about a screw axis. Axial capture: fillister head on the block top through a #8 normal-clearance hole; 38.1 mm stock less the 25.232 mm raised block gives 12.868 mm nominal insertion (13.378 mm maximum) into the transferred through-tapped #8-32 UNC-2B deck seat; the 13 mm nominal skin limits the minimum checked source-model full-form overlap to 10.10 mm; any exit protrusion is into pocket air.
- `north-pivot-bracket-hold-down`: clamps the north pivot_bracket foot to the stationary rocker_arm_support rail (neither moves in operation); one screw per foot, but the bracket cannot turn about it: the pivot shaft runs through both brackets' ear bores, so either bracket's yaw is reacted by the shaft and the other bracket's screw, and the rocker bank's oscillating drag acts about the pivot-shaft axis (Z), perpendicular to the vertical screw axis. Axial capture: fillister head on the bracket foot top face; thread in a bottoming-tapped #8-32 2B blind seat transferred through the foot.
- `south-pivot-bracket-hold-down`: clamps the south pivot_bracket foot to the stationary rocker_arm_support rail (neither moves in operation); one screw per foot, but the bracket cannot turn about it: the pivot shaft runs through both brackets' ear bores, so either bracket's yaw is reacted by the shaft and the other bracket's screw, and the rocker bank's oscillating drag acts about the pivot-shaft axis (Z), perpendicular to the vertical screw axis (the arms float, so nothing thrusts on the ear, #3114). Axial capture: fillister head on the bracket foot top face; thread in a bottoming-tapped #8-32 2B blind seat transferred through the span-set foot.
- `fulcrum-keeper-screw-in-top-frame`: clamps each fulcrum_keeper foot to the stationary top_frame rail (neither moves in operation); one screw per foot, but the keeper cannot turn about it: the fulcrum_shaft runs through both keepers' bores, each set screw (MHA-CH-VN-55 on its flat) clamping it to its bore, so the two keepers and the shaft are one frame held by two screws 177.3 apart on the shaft line, and either keeper's yaw about its own screw would have to drag the other keeper's clamped foot sideways; the channel_levers' oscillating drag acts about the shaft axis (Z), perpendicular to the vertical screw axis, and their spring and bar loads press the foot on its seat. Axial capture: slotted fillister head seated flush in the keeper foot counterbore (CBORE_DEPTH 2.1082); thread in top_frame KeeperTaps #2-56 x 7.6 thread / 11.5 drill blind, transferred from the foot hole at fit-up.
- `channel-anchor-in-summing-plate`: per-channel calibration unscrews each anchor to set its spring preload (5% of preload per turn); the anchor also rides the rocking summing lever under a channel-spring load that cycles with the crank, and that spring pulls the anchor out of its tap. Axial capture: the trimmed 9489T111 shank crosses the full 7.00 uniform coefficient-plate tap, whose thread bears the spring's upward pull; the Ø3.175 neck shoulders on the plate top only when fully seated (calibration backs it off up to 1 counted turn), and at the worst case (shortest in-band part, thickest in-band plate, whole budget backed out) full thread still spans the plate.
- `magnifying-bracket-screws`: clamp the bracket flange to the summing lever; both rock together about the knife edge without relative motion at the seat. Two screws react the bracket moment as a shear couple, not operating spin friction about either screw axis. Axial capture: fillister heads on the bracket flange front face; shanks pass normal #2 clearance into the lever's blind bottoming #2-56 seats.
