## Print route — decision pending

| route | min order | unit cost | lead time | ships from | notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Print-on-demand (Lulu / Blurb) | 1 | high | days | multiple regions | zero inventory risk, worst margin, best for a small campaign |
| IngramSpark | 1 | medium | ~2 weeks | US/UK/AU | POD with real distribution; ISBN needed |
| Short offset run | ~500+ | low | 6–10 weeks | one origin | best unit cost, **worst risk** — pay up front, ship worldwide yourself |

For a first campaign with an unknown backer count, POD is almost always right
even though the margin is bad: it converts an inventory gamble into a
per-unit cost.

## Costs the budget must include and campaigns routinely forget

- Kickstarter fee 5 % + payment processing ~3–5 %
- Failed/dropped pledges (budget 3–8 % of the funded total)
- Taxes on the funded amount — **treat the raise as income, not as a budget**
- Packaging materials and the time to pack
- Returns, damage in transit, re-sends
- The survey/address-collection tool (BackerKit or similar) if used
- Currency conversion on a campaign raised in USD and spent in BRL

## Sequence

1. Campaign closes → 14 days for pledges to settle
2. Backer survey for addresses and print-edition sizes
3. Names locked for the acknowledgements tier → book to print
4. Digital rewards ship on publication day
5. Physical rewards ship in waves by zone
6. Post-mortem into [`../../ai-story/`](../../ai-story/) — the campaign is part
   of the story
