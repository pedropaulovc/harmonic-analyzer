# Timeline

> Dates are placeholders. Fill them from the logbook's actual pace once a few

## Phase A — before launch (audience first, campaign second)

| week | milestone | gate |
|---|---|---|
| A-12 | Landing page live, email capture running | — |
| A-10 | Logbook public and updating weekly | ≥ 4 real entries |
| A-8 | Book outline stable; two sample chapters drafted | a reader can judge the writing |
| A-6 | Campaign video shot | needs real machining footage |
| A-4 | Print quotes in hand; tiers locked | `rewards/fulfilment.md` complete |
| A-2 | Page submitted to Kickstarter review | — |
| A-1 | Pre-launch page live; email list warmed | ≥ 500 signups |
| A-0 | **Launch** | — |

**The gate that matters:** do not launch before there is real machining
footage and a real logbook. The pitch is "someone is actually doing this",
and CAD renders alone cannot carry it.

## Phase B — the campaign (30 days)

| day | activity |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | The decisive window. Email list first, then communities. |
| 3–7 | Daily updates: one part, one operation, one photograph. |
| 8–25 | The trough. Keep shipping updates; this is where consistency wins. |
| 26–30 | Final push. Stretch goals if funded. |

Communities worth a genuine (non-spammy) post: `r/machinists`,
`r/metalworking`, `r/hobbycnc`, Practical Machinist, Home Model Engine
Machinist, Hacker News, Hackaday tip line, the model-engineering forums.
**Participate before launch, not on launch day.**

## Phase C — build and write

Sequenced by the [curriculum](../../logbook/curriculum/README.md), because the
skills gate the parts:

| stage | parts | blocked on |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Frame, base, simple brackets | M01–M05 (measurement → milling) |
| 2 | Shafts, bushings, arbors | M02–M03 (turning, boring, parting to length) |
| 3 | Rocker arms, connecting rods, amplitude bars | M04–M06 (profile milling, drilling) |
| 4 | **Cone + cylinder gears** — the long pole | M07–M08 (indexing, gear cutting) |
| 5 | Summing lever + knife edge | M09 + a fabrication decision |
| 6 | Magnifier, pen, paper drive | M03–M06 |
| 7 | Assembly, calibration, first output | everything |

Manuscript chapters are written **as each stage completes**, not at the end.
